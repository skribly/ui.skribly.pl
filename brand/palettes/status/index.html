Product palette

Skribly Status palette

The colour of Skribly Status in the three themes, its contrast on every surface, and the accent, surface and status colours its interface shares with every Skribly product.
All product palettes

Product colourLink to the section Product colour

The token --sk-tool-status colours the accent block of the mark, borders, tints and the brand patterns of Skribly Status. It must reach 3:1 on every surface in the light and dark themes and 4.5:1 in high contrast, the rule for controls and graphics; test/tokens.test.ts checks it. Text in the name of a product uses the text colour, not the product colour.
