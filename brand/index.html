Brand

The Skribly brand

One mark for every product: an accent block, an ink block with a glyph cut into it, two bars and two dots, like a page of a notebook. A product differs from the others by its glyph and its colour, and by nothing else.

The markLink to the section The mark

The mark is drawn on a 64 unit grid. The accent block takes the product colour; the ink block, the bars and the first dot take the text colour of the theme; the second dot is the product colour at 55 percent. The glyph is a 2.4 unit stroke with round ends, cut out of the ink block.

  1. Accent block, in the product colour
  2. Ink block with the glyph of the product
  3. Two bars, the lines of a page
  4. Two dots, in the product colour

Every productLink to the section Every product

Eighteen products have a mark. Each links to its palette, with its colour in the three themes and the contrast of that colour on every surface.
