Brand hero

The ground of a Skribly home hero: the brand pattern, the sky that follows the theme, and the moving agents with their Pause motion button. Every part is a kit component for Astro, every word a property.

The brand heroLink to the section The brand hero

ProductHero variant="brand" takes HeroBackdrop in its backdrop slot and HeroAgents as its media: a page of code scrolls behind the Agents panel, and each agent moves from working to waiting to done.

From a task in the tracker to a commit you reviewed

Agents work side by side, each in its own worktree. You decide what ships.

The skyLink to the section The sky

HeroSky shows the sun in the light theme and the moon with a few stars in the dark one, and follows the theme switch at once, the system theme included. In the high contrast theme and in forced colours it is drawn as outlines.

The moving agentsLink to the section The moving agents

Motion runs only with JavaScript and with motion allowed. The still frame already shows all three states. The Pause motion button stops every moving part of the page, and every Skribly site remembers the choice.

Patterned bandsLink to the section Patterned bands

Band is a section on a ground: plain or sunken, with a brand pattern in the product colour and a rule in that colour along its top. A home page alternates plain sections with bands. The product is a class, never an inline style.

A dotted board

Text on a band keeps at least 4.5:1 in every theme.

A grid board

Text on a band keeps at least 4.5:1 in every theme.

Corner linkLink to the section Corner link

CornerLink floats in the bottom right corner of every page above the back to top button, with the kit's one gap between them. Its small button hides it for the rest of the visit. It comes after the footer in the tab order, has a visible focus and is left out in print; a site leaves it out on the page it links to. Below it stands still in the page, as an example.

Newsletter sign-upLink to the section Newsletter sign-up

NewsletterSignup puts the newsletter on any Skribly site: a band in the newsletter colour with a title, one line, a large e-mail field and a button. It is a plain GET form to newsletter.skribly.pl (or /pl/), which opens with the address filled in its own sign-up form; the person chooses the topics and gives consent there, so nothing is sent from this band. Every word is a property, and the site allows https://newsletter.skribly.pl in form-action.

Get the newsletter by e-mail

AI news worth reading once a week, with a source under every item. Free and without tracking.

You confirm the topics and your consent on the newsletter site.

RulesLink to the section Rules

  • Decoration is hidden from assistive technology and never takes a click.
  • Nothing is an image the page waits for: the sky and the code page are inline SVG, the patterns are CSS.
  • Motion that lasts longer than five seconds has the Pause motion button, and its focus is visible.
  • With less motion, without JavaScript or while paused, the page shows the still frame.
  • In the high contrast theme, in forced colours and in print the patterns and the code page drop out.
